🔒 Privacy Policy — EDC Lumen Log

Effective date: 2026-05-24
Last updated: 2026-05-24
App: EDC Lumen Log (Android, package com.masakimadeinjapan.edc_flashlight)
Publisher: Masaki, doing business as Masaki Made in Japan (Singapore)

TL;DR

  • This app does not collect personal data about you.
  • The app runs fully offline. No analytics. No tracking. No ad SDK (no AdMob).
  • The only outbound network calls happen when you tap an Amazon affiliate link, which opens Amazon in your browser. Amazon then collects data directly under Amazon's privacy policy — not ours.
  • Your favorites and compare cart are stored only on your device, in a local SQLite database.

1. Who we are

EDC Lumen Log ("the app", "we") is a free Android application built by Masaki, a solo developer based in Singapore, publishing under the brand Masaki Made in Japan. The app is distributed via Google Play Store and provides offline reviews of EDC flashlights. The Android publisher account is com.masakimadeinjapan.*.

Contact for privacy questions, deletion requests, or any concern: mk.madeinjapan@gmail.com (typical response time: 1-3 business days).

2. Data we do NOT collect

To be explicit, the Android app does not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, IP address, device ID)
  • Usage analytics (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Analytics, no Crashlytics in v1.0)
  • Crash reports
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, files, or any other device content
  • Audio or sensor data
  • Advertising ID (the app does not include any ad SDK)

About this website (this privacy.html page when served on the web): This Privacy Policy is also served as a static HTML page on GitHub Pages (masakki55.github.io), which is GitHub's static hosting service. When you visit this website, GitHub (the hosting provider) may log standard HTTP request data — including your IP address, user-agent string, and referrer — for the purpose of operating, securing, and troubleshooting their hosting service. This logging is performed by GitHub, not by us, and is governed by GitHub's General Privacy Statement and GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not place any analytics scripts, cookies, or trackers on this website ourselves — no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no advertising tags, no third-party fonts/CDN requiring cookies. The Android app itself runs offline and is not affected by this website-hosting disclosure.

3. Data stored locally on your device

The app stores the following data only on your device, in an app-private SQLite database:

  • Favorites: List of flashlight IDs you marked as favorite
  • Compare cart: Up to 2 (free) or 3 (premium) flashlight IDs you added for side-by-side comparison
  • Settings: Locale (EN / JP), theme mode, premium purchase status

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data (Android Settings → Apps → EDC Lumen Log → Storage → Clear data) removes it all.

4. Third-party services

4.1 Amazon Affiliate Links

When you tap a Buy link in the app or on this website, the link opens Amazon's website (Amazon.com / Amazon.co.jp / Amazon.sg) in your default browser via Android's standard intent system or your computer's browser. We embed an affiliate tag in the URL so we earn a small commission if you complete a purchase.

The app itself does not send your data to Amazon — it only hands off the URL to your browser. However, once you arrive at Amazon, Amazon collects data about you directly under Amazon's own privacy policy (e.g. cookies, IP address, account info if logged in, purchase history). We have no insight into and no control over what Amazon collects. Amazon's policies:

  • Amazon US Privacy Notice
  • Amazon JP Privacy Notice
  • Amazon SG Privacy Notice

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. See About / Methodology for our approach to honest reviews and the policy of not accepting vendor money.

4.2 Google Play Billing (Premium Purchase)

If you choose to purchase the Premium upgrade (¥370 / S$3 one-time, unlocks 3-light compare), the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing, a service of Google operated by Google directly. Payment data is collected and processed by Google, not by the app or by Masaki Made in Japan. The app only receives a binary success/failure signal from Google after the transaction — we do not see your payment method, card number, billing address, or any personal details. Google Play's privacy policy applies to the transaction:

  • Google Privacy Policy

4.3 No other third-party services

v1.0 of the app does not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Crashlytics, AdMob, Facebook SDK, or any other third-party data collection service. If a future version adds any (e.g. Crashlytics for crash diagnostics), this Privacy Policy will be updated before the change ships, the "Last updated" date will change, and Google Play release notes will surface the change prominently.

5. Children's privacy

The app is rated for general audiences and does not target children. The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age. Because no personal data is collected at all, the app is incidentally compliant with COPPA (US), GDPR-K (EU), Children's Privacy Code (Singapore PDPA Children's Code 2023), and Japan APPI Article 17 treatment of children's data.

6. Data retention

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to retain. Your local app data (favorites, settings, compare cart) is retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app data via Android Settings → Apps → EDC Lumen Log → Storage → Clear data.

7. Your rights (regional applicability)

7.1 EU / EEA / UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, and port your personal data. Since this app does not collect personal data, there is no data for us to provide or delete on your behalf. To remove the data the app stores locally on your device, clear app data via Android Settings → Apps → EDC Lumen Log → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. If you tap an Amazon affiliate link and later want Amazon to remove data Amazon collected about you, please contact Amazon directly via their privacy contact.

7.2 California (CCPA / CPRA)

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising purposes. We do not collect personal information through this app at all.

7.3 Singapore (PDPA 2012, amended 2020)

We comply with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act. Because the app does not collect any personal data, no Notification, Consent, or Access/Correction obligations arise from app usage itself. Inquiries: mk.madeinjapan@gmail.com.

7.4 Japan (改正個人情報保護法 / Act on the Protection of Personal Information)

本アプリは個人情報を収集しないため、 個人情報保護法 (改正法含む) における主要規定 (利用目的の特定・第三者提供制限・開示請求への対応等) は、 本アプリの利用に関しては適用されません。 ただし Amazon アフィリエイトリンクをタップした後、 Amazon が独自に取得する個人データについては Amazon の Privacy Notice が適用されます。 問い合わせ先: mk.madeinjapan@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy (e.g., adding AdMob banners or analytics in a future version), we will update the "Last updated" date at the top, post a release note in Google Play, and — for material changes — surface an in-app notification on the next launch. The policy applies to the version of the app you are using; previous versions are archived in this repository under git history.

9. AI-Assisted Content Disclosure

The flashlight reviews, articles, and recommendations distributed in the Android app and on this website were drafted with AI assistance (Claude AI by Anthropic). All factual claims (lumens, candela, throw, IP rating, prices) are sourced from manufacturer pages and community-verified sources. All source URLs are visible in the app's "Sources" section per flashlight and in each review's footer. Reviews ("verdicts") reflect honest critique, including explicit limitation disclosure. Independent third-party audit (Codex CLI by OpenAI) is run against primary sources before publication.
