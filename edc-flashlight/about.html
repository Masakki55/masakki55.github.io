📖 About EDC Lumen Log

This page explains how flashlights are selected, how scores are assigned, and why this project does not accept vendor money.

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Some Buy links on this site and inside the companion Android app are Amazon affiliate links (Amazon.com / Amazon.co.jp / Amazon.sg). Reviews are not paid or sponsored by manufacturers.

The problem this project solves

If you've ever tried to pick an EDC flashlight, you know the pain:

  • YouTube "best of 2026" lists are 90% sponsored
  • Spec sheets lie ("9800mAh 18650"? Impossible — Li-ion chemistry caps at ~3500mAh)
  • Reddit r/flashlight is great but search is hard, threads scatter
  • 1Lumen and Liteshow reviews are detailed but English-only
  • Japanese / Singaporean buyers have to mentally translate every spec

EDC Lumen Log condenses all that into one offline app (and this companion review site), with honest verdicts and source URLs you can verify yourself.

Methodology

1. Light selection (60-75 in v1.0)

The curated list is built from 4 source pools:

After deduplication, the v1.0 launch list has 60-75 unique lights. v1.1 will expand to 100+.

2. 4-Axis Rating

Each light gets four scores (1-5):

  • Build — anodizing quality, knurling, threading, water resistance, drop resistance
  • Runtime — sustained brightness vs claimed, battery capacity actually delivered, thermal regulation
  • Pocketability — weight, length, clip design, in-pocket comfort during 8-hour carry
  • Value — price vs comparable alternatives in the same category

3. Verdict voice ("Toyota Camry of flashlights")

Reviews are written in a deliberately honest voice. We:

  • Compare specs to reality ("spec sheet vs reality")
  • Disclose limitations even on "good" lights ("8 months daily carry — cool-white tint only, no high-CRI option")
  • Flag drugstore-tier products clearly ("Replace with Sofirn SP10 Pro ASAP")
  • Never use AI marketing-speak ("game-changer", "ultimate", "amazing breakdown" — banned phrases)
"Toyota Camry of EDC. 2000 lumens, Anduril UI, USB-C, IPX8 at the budget tier — boring spec sheet, dependable carry. Tint is cool-white, knurling sharp." — Verdict on the Sofirn SC31 Pro (check current price via the regional Amazon links in the full review)

4. Source citations

Every light has at least one source URL (manufacturer page, 1Lumen review, or Reddit thread). Every article has citations. You can verify any claim by tapping through to the source.

5. Updates

Specs and reviews are refreshed quarterly. The "Last verified" date is visible per light. If a date is older than 180 days, the app shows an "outdated" warning so you can re-check before buying.

6. AI-assisted drafting, human-audited

Reviews are drafted with AI assistance (Claude AI by Anthropic) and audited by a separate third-party CLI tool (Codex by OpenAI) against the listed primary sources. All factual claims (lumens, candela, prices, runtime numbers) are traceable to manufacturer pages or independent reviewer measurements via source URLs. This disclosure is repeated in the app's Privacy Policy §9.

What this project does NOT do

  • ❌ Take vendor money. No "sponsored" reviews.
  • ❌ Display ads in v1.0. (Premium upgrade unlocks the 3rd compare slot — that's the only direct app monetization beyond Amazon affiliate.)
  • ❌ Collect personal data (see Privacy Policy)
  • ❌ Use "best of 2026" or "top 10 of all time" clickbait. We use concrete use-case framings ("Best AAA keychain light", "Best budget-tier 21700 EDC", "Best high-CRI 18650 for warm tint").

What this project DOES do

  • ✅ Earn small commission from Amazon when you buy via the affiliate links (typical 1-4% of purchase)
  • ✅ Sell a one-time ¥370 / S$3 Premium upgrade in the Android app, unlocking 3-light compare (vs free 2-light)
  • ✅ Publish all reviews in English + Japanese (in the app; this website is English-only for now)
  • ✅ Disclose AI-assisted writing (see Privacy Policy §9)
  • ✅ Disclose Amazon affiliate participation in compliance with FTC 16 CFR Part 255 (US), Japan 景品表示法, and Singapore CASE guidelines

Why "Lumen Log"?

Because we log lumens, not hype. Every review tells you what the light actually outputs, what it actually costs, and what it actually feels like in the pocket after 8 hours.
