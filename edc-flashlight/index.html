FAQ

Why don't you just review on YouTube?

YouTube reviews are 90% sponsored. The economics force creators to push high-margin tactical lights instead of the budget-tier light that's actually better in the pocket. An app-based review keeps revenue separated from review content (the app is free; affiliate is opt-in by the reader).

How do you measure lumens / candela?

I don't have a calibrated integrating sphere. For measured values I cross-reference 1Lumen.com, ZeroAir, and BLF threads (community-trusted measurement sources). For claimed values I quote the manufacturer spec sheet. Both are presented side-by-side per review so you can see when they diverge.

What's an "EDC" flashlight?

Every Day Carry — a flashlight small enough to pocket every day. Usually under 130g, under 120mm length, with a clip. The opposite of a "tactical" or "search and rescue" light (which are heavier, brighter, and designed for specific high-stress use cases).

What gets a "do not buy" rating?

Anything with fake specs (Ultrafire's 9800mAh claim is the canonical example), drugstore-tier IPX4 in 2026, or known fire safety issues. We don't soften "do not buy" verdicts — see the Ultrafire warning.
